The CLI links the unmodified official parser through Cargo. No registry source,
native binary, system library or official Hurl executable is redistributed in
the source archive. Building downloads dependencies under their own licenses.
CI records the complete resolved dependency versions, registry checksums and
SPDX license expressions in evidence/native-provenance.json and the exact lock
in evidence/resolved-Cargo.lock. The provenance stage rejects unreviewed license
identifiers before compiling. libxml2 is a system build/runtime dependency under
its own upstream terms; no libxml2 copy is bundled.

Official Hurl and hurlfmt 8.0.1 release executables are used only by the disposable
synthetic test harness. Their tarball digest is pinned in scripts/upstream-pin.json.
They are excluded from source and verification artifacts.
